Directions & Guest Guide

Student Recreation Center North · UC Riverside · June 6, 2026

Getting There

Step-by-Step for Guests

Follow these steps for a smooth, stress-free morning

1

Plan Your Route

The ceremony is at the Student Recreation Center North, Main Gymnasium — UC Riverside, 900 University Ave, Riverside, CA 92521. Allow extra travel time on ceremony day.

Google Maps Apple Maps
2

Parking

Parking is available in any UCR visitor lot with a paid permit. Lot 24 is the closest to the venue — but it fills up fast, so arrive early. Lot 26 is a good backup option nearby. Visit transportation.ucr.edu/visitor-parking to plan ahead.

Lot 24 on Google Maps Lot 24 on Apple Maps
Lot 26 on Google Maps Lot 26 on Apple Maps
3

Arrive Early — Security Checkpoint

Security gates open at 7:30 AM. All guests pass through metal detectors and bag checks. Give yourself plenty of time — lines may be long before the 8:00 AM door opening. Avoid bringing large bags if possible.

4

Have Your Ticket Ready

Every guest needs a ticket — have it on your phone or printed before you reach the checkpoint. Children under 2 don't need a ticket but must stay on a lap throughout the ceremony.

5

Find Your Seat

Venue doors open at 8:00 AM. Follow the signage inside — ushers will guide you to seating. Graduates enter in a separate procession at 9:00 AM.

6

After the Ceremony — Lunch!

The ceremony wraps up by noon and we'll be heading to The Old Spaghetti Factory for lunch at 12:30 PM. Don't forget to share your photos in the shared photo album!
