What is a Hooding Ceremony?

The hooding ceremony is one of the most meaningful traditions in graduate education — and one of the most personal moments of Kevin's entire academic journey.

During the ceremony, Kevin will walk across the stage where his faculty advisor, Dr. Brian Collier, will drape the hood over his shoulders — a personal, one-on-one moment that sets the hooding apart from any other graduation tradition. When Kevin's name is called, it marks his official entry into the global community of scholars.

The doctoral hood traces its origins to medieval European universities, where robes and hoods were everyday academic dress. Over centuries, the hood evolved into a symbol of scholarly achievement — its colors, lining, and velvet trim now encoding the graduate's university and field of study.
